Privacy policy

FitMe (FitForMe on the App Store), by Nadav Krashin. Last updated November 14, 2026.

FitMe keeps your closet on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sold. This page lists exactly what leaves your iPhone, where it goes and why.

What stays on your iPhone

Your clothes and their photos, outfits you save, trips, what you wore, and what FitMe learns from your reactions are stored only on your iPhone, and in its iCloud or computer backups if you make them. Deleting the app deletes them. Calendar event titles are read on the iPhone to suggest what to dress for and never leave it.

Claude, by Anthropic (only if you allow it)

FitMe asks before sending anything to Claude, Anthropic's AI, and you can turn it off in Settings → AI. When it's on:

  • Photos of clothes you add, and the product page you choose with Find online, are sent to name each piece and tag its colors, fit and fabric.
  • When you ask the stylist or check a bag, text descriptions of your clothes, the occasion and the weather are sent. No photos.

These requests pass through FitMe's server on Cloudflare, which forwards them to Anthropic and doesn't keep their content. Anthropic handles them under its commercial terms and privacy policy.

Usage counts and app verification

To give each iPhone its monthly AI allowance, FitMe's server stores a random key that identifies this copy of the app (created with Apple's App Attest) and how many requests it made this month. It contains nothing about you and isn't linked to your name, Apple ID or any other data.

Weather

Your approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to Open-Meteo to get today's weather. For trips, the cities you enter are sent to Open-Meteo for forecasts.

Find online

Find online opens a web browser inside FitMe. The search uses DuckDuckGo with the words you type, and the shop sites you visit see your visit as in any browser.

Camera, photos and calendar

FitMe uses only the photos you take or choose, and reads your calendar only after you allow it. You can change these permissions anytime in the iPhone's Settings.

Children

FitMe isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.

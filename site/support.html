Adding clothes

In Closet, tap + and take photos one after another, or choose them from your library. Each photo is cut out and tagged, then waits in the inbox for you to check. With Claude turned off, you fill in the tags yourself.

"This month's AI allowance is used up"

FitMe includes a monthly allowance of Claude tagging and stylist requests per iPhone. It resets on the 1st. Until then, everything else works and you can tag clothes by hand.

Turning Claude on or off

Settings → AI → Use Claude. See the privacy policy for what it receives.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your closet lives on your iPhone and moves with an iCloud or computer backup. There's no account and no sync between devices yet.
